Guest mode

By default, you will play in guest mode.

When using guest mode, your progress will be saved locally in your browser.

It will not be accessible when changing device and can be lost if you clear your browser cookies or browsing history.

Account creation

You register using the form in the top left menu.

Upon registering, you will be automatically logged into your newly created account.

Login

When logged in, your progress will be saved into the cloud.

You can access it on any device as long as you log into your account.

When logging in, the game will restart and load data saved into the cloud.

Logout

When you log out, the game will restart and you will be back to using guest mode.

Forgot password

If you ever forget your password, you can recover it using the "forgot password?" button on the top left menu.

Note that this will only work if the email you registered with is a valid email address.

Extra features

When logged in to your account, you will have access to extra features like profile, leaderboards, achievements.
